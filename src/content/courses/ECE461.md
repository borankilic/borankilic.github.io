A senior/first-year-graduate course on the theory of digital communication: geometric signal space, matched filtering and optimal (ML/MAP) detection, digital modulation schemes and their error rates, and the information-theoretic limits of reliable communication. My course project on OTFS modulation grew out of this class. Taken on exchange at UIUC.
